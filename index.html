How the math works

A block at difficulty D costs D × 232 hashes on average. Your wait is that work divided by your hashrate: 

solo wait      = difficulty × 2³² ÷ hashrate
blocks per day = hashrate × 86,400 ÷ (difficulty × 2³²)
subsidy / day  = blocks per day × block subsidy

This is independent of how fast the rest of the network is currently producing blocks. Faster network block times do not change your expected hashes per block — only the live difficulty does.
